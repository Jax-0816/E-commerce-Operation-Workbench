# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful community. Participate in good faith, be considerate of differing experience levels, and give feedback about ideas and work rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or take other appropriate action to protect participants.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's private contact channel. Reports will be handled as discreetly as possible.
